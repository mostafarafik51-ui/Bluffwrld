# Credits for the films in this folder

- **Main character and crowd base model** — `js/models/michelle.glb`, a Mixamo character (Adobe), from the original OpenWorldJS project.
- **Male crowd model** — `js/models/man.glb`, the Ready Player Me sample avatar shipped with the three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/readyplayer.me.glb`, three.js is MIT-licensed). Check Ready Player Me's terms before commercial use.
- **Voices** — made with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) text-to-speech:
  - `en_US-hfc_female-medium`, `en_US-hfc_male-medium` (Hi-Fi Captain dataset)
  - `en_US-libritts_r-medium` — trained on LibriTTS-R, **CC BY 4.0**: credit "Voices: Piper / LibriTTS-R (CC BY 4.0)".
- **Music and sound effects** — synthesised from scratch by `ad/sound.py`; no samples.
